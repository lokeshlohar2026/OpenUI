DATABASE SCHEMA & RELATIONSHIP MAP (Live PostgreSQL Engine - 28 Tables):

TABLE RELATIONSHIPS & JOIN GRAPH (HOW TABLES CONNECT):
• mfi360_funds (Central Mutual Fund Master):
  - JOIN mfi360_amcs ON mfi360_funds.mf_id = mfi360_amcs.mf_id (Fund House / AMC)
  - JOIN mfi360_fund_plans ON mfi360_funds.fund_id = mfi360_fund_plans.fund_id (Direct/Regular Plans, Expense Ratios, Exit Loads, ISIN)
  - JOIN mfi360_fund_portfolio_holdings ON mfi360_funds.fund_id = mfi360_fund_portfolio_holdings.fund_id (Monthly Stock & Debt Holdings)
  - JOIN mfi360_fund_aum_history ON mfi360_funds.fund_id = mfi360_fund_aum_history.fund_id (Historical AUM Growth Timeline)
  - JOIN mfi360_scheme_valuation_metrics ON mfi360_funds.fund_id = mfi360_scheme_valuation_metrics.fund_id (Portfolio P/E, P/B, Dividend Yield, Market Cap)
  - JOIN mfi360_fund_debt_metrics ON mfi360_funds.fund_id = mfi360_fund_debt_metrics.fund_id (Average Maturity, Duration, YTM)
  - JOIN mfi360_fund_manager_tenures ON mfi360_funds.fund_id = mfi360_fund_manager_tenures.fund_id (Scheme Management Tenures)

• mfi360_fund_plans (Scheme Plan Variants & Performance):
  - JOIN mfi360_scheme_risk_ratios ON mfi360_fund_plans.scheme_id = mfi360_scheme_risk_ratios.scheme_id (Risk Ratios: Sharpe, Beta, Standard Deviation)
  - JOIN mfi360_fund_plan_dividend_history ON mfi360_fund_plans.scheme_id = mfi360_fund_plan_dividend_history.scheme_id (Dividend Distribution History)
  - JOIN mfi360_fund_plans_nav_history ON mfi360_fund_plans.scheme_id = mfi360_fund_plans_nav_history.scheme_id (Daily Historical NAVs)
  - JOIN amfi_isin_master ON mfi360_fund_plans.isin = amfi_isin_master.isin_primary (AMFI Scheme Metadata)
  - JOIN bse_isin_master ON mfi360_fund_plans.isin = bse_isin_master.isin (BSE Product Metadata)

• mfi360_fund_manager_tenures (Manager Tenures):
  - JOIN mfi360_fund_managers ON mfi360_fund_manager_tenures.fund_manager_id = mfi360_fund_managers.fund_manager_id (Manager Qualifications & Bio)

• Taxonomy & Category Benchmarks:
  - amfi_sub_categories: Links sub_category_id to sub_category_name and category_name (Equity, Debt, Hybrid, Solution Oriented, Other)
  - amfi_fund_benchmarks: Links sub_category_id to scheme_name and mandated benchmark name
  - benchmark_reference_map: Resolves benchmark string across MFI360, AMFI portal, and MCX

• Market Indices & Commodity Trackers:
  - mfi360_indices_history JOIN mfi360_indices ON mfi360_indices_history.index_id = mfi360_indices.index_id
  - amfi_portal_indices_history JOIN amfi_portal_indices ON amfi_portal_indices_history.index_id = amfi_portal_indices.index_id
  - mcx_icomdex_indices_history JOIN mcx_icomdex_indices ON mcx_icomdex_indices_history.instrument_identifier = mcx_icomdex_indices.instrument_identifier
  - mcx_spot_market_prices JOIN mcx_spot_commodities ON mcx_spot_market_prices.commodity_id = mcx_spot_commodities.commodity_id
  - mfi360_composite_benchmark_history (Custom composite benchmarks tracked by composite_key)
